The Deuteronomy experience

Faith you can put into practice.

From Moses’ leadership to the Shema’s call to love God wholeheartedly, this proposed gathering connects Scripture with everyday choices. Volunteer-led sessions explore worship, leadership, generosity, justice, and community through discussion, drama, music, and making.

The proposed program

Two days. A shared journey.

This overview follows the original uploaded website draft. The alternate “Choose Life” brochures in Resources describe a different schedule.

Day 1 · 8:30 AM

Worship & Deuteronomy 1–3

Kick off with live Christian hip-hop and a Q&A with Moses, Joshua, and more!

Day 1 · 10:00 AM

God’s Law (Deut. 4–6:9)

Hear Scripture with live beats, Streetlights-style, and learn how to live it.

Day 1 · 11:00 AM–3:30 PM

Breakouts

Choose from Psalm 119 poetry, leadership challenges, or carving Deut. verses.

Day 2 · 8:30 AM

Shema: One God

Chant Deut. 6:4–9 and craft a ‘mezuzah’ to keep God first.

Day 2 · 10:00 AM–2:00 PM

Breakouts

Dive into social justice, king training, or geography with hands-on fun.

Day 2 · 2:30 PM

Moses’ Farewell

End with a dramatic retelling of Deut. 31–34 and a spoken-word wrap-up.

Learn by doing

Find your workshop.

The original plan offers up to three concurrent workshops in each breakout block.

Psalm 119: Love Letter

Write a love letter to God’s Word with street-art vibes.

Leadership with Jethro

Role-play as leaders in a ‘Survivor: Desert Edition’ challenge.

Tablet Carving

Carve Deut. verses into clay like ancient scribes, TikTok-style.

Geography of Deuteronomy

Tour Moab and build 3D Jordan River models.

Blessings & Cursings

Act out choices in skits based on Deut. 28.

Money Matters

Play a budgeting game to learn tithing (Deut. 15).

Festival Planning

Plan a modern Passover party with menus and playlists.

How to Govern

Role-play as judges using Deut. 16, like a true-crime podcast.

Song of Moses

Write and perform rap verses inspired by Deut. 32.

King Prep School

Train like a godly king with a leadership obstacle course.

Justice League

Create action plans for issues like poverty (Deut. 24).
