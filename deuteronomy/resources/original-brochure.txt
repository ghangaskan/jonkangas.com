## Inside Left Panel: What’s in Store?
**Why Deuteronomy?**  
This ancient book is your guide to living boldly for God. From Moses’ epic leadership to the Shema’s call to love God fully, we’ll unpack how Deuteronomy’s truths hit home for teens today. Expect music, skits, crafts, and real talk that make Scripture come alive.

**Day 1 Highlights**:  
- **Worship & Deuteronomy 1–3**: Kick off with live Christian hip-hop and a Q&A with actors as Moses, Joshua, and more, sharing Israel’s wild journey.  
- **God’s Law (Deut. 4–6:9)**: Hear Scripture like a Streetlights audio Bible with live beats, plus a talk on living your best life for God.  
- **Breakout Sessions**: Choose from workshops like crafting clay tablets, planning a biblical festival, or rapping the Song of Moses Eminem-style.

**Day 2 Highlights**:  
- **Shema: One God**: Start with a hype chant of Deut. 6:4–9 and craft a “mezuzah” to keep God first.  
- **More Breakouts**: Dive into leadership, social justice, or geography with hands-on activities like building 3D Jordan River models.  
- **Moses’ Farewell**: Close with a dramatic retelling of Deut. 31–34, reflecting on your legacy with a spoken-word wrap-up.

## Inside Center Panel: Breakout Sessions
**Pick your vibe!** Choose from up to three workshops per session, designed for all learning styles: visual, auditory, kinesthetic, and reading/writing.  

- **Psalm 119: A Love Letter to Deuteronomy**: Write a “love letter” to God’s Word with street-art word clouds.  
- **Jethro and the Sons of Korah Teach Leadership**: Role-play as leaders in a “Survivor: Desert Edition” challenge.  
- **Tablet Carving and Plaster Inscribing**: Carve Deut. verses into clay like ancient scribes, with a TikTok-style twist.  
- **The Geography of Deuteronomy**: Take a virtual tour of Moab and the Jordan River, building 3D models.  
- **Blessings and Cursings**: Act out modern choices in skits, exploring Deut. 28’s consequences.  
- **Money Matters: Tithing and Debts**: Play a budgeting game to learn generosity like Deut. 15.  
- **Party Planning: Festivals**: Plan a modern Passover party with menus and playlists.  
- **How to Govern**: Role-play as judges using Deut. 16, tackling tough decisions like a true-crime podcast.  
- **Song of Moses: R&B Styled Like Eminem**: Write and perform rap verses inspired by Deut. 32.  
- **King Prep School**: Train like a godly king with a leadership obstacle course (Deut. 17).  
- **Justice League: Social Justice**: Create action plans for issues like poverty, inspired by Deut. 24.  

**All sessions are led by passionate volunteers who make the Bible dope and relatable!**
