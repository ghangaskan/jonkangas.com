# Conference Proposal: Biblical Study of Deuteronomy for Teenage Christians

## Conference Overview
This two-day conference, designed for teenage Christians (ages 13–18), brings the Book of Deuteronomy to life through engaging, interactive, and creative sessions. By exploring its historical, spiritual, and practical dimensions, we connect the ancient text to the lives of modern teens, fostering a deeper understanding of God's Word and its relevance today. The conference combines storytelling, music, interactive workshops, and dynamic teaching to inspire faith, leadership, and worship. Each day includes combined sessions, breakout sessions (no more than three concurrently), and meals featuring teen-favorite and biblically themed foods. Volunteers run the conference, and participant fees cover food and craft supplies. Facilitator guidance is provided to ensure sessions are edgy, relevant, and impactful for teens.

## Two-Day Conference Schedule

### Day 1
**8:30 AM – 9:30 AM: Combined Session – Worship: All In & Deuteronomy 1–3 (60 minutes)**  
**Format**: Live worship and Q&A panel with storytelling  
**Description**: Open with a high-energy worship session inspired by Deuteronomy’s call to wholehearted devotion (Deut. 6:5), featuring live music with a mix of contemporary Christian hip-hop and acoustic vibes to grab teens’ attention. Transition into a Q&A panel with volunteer actors portraying Moses, Joshua, Caleb, and Aaron, sharing vivid, first-person retellings of Deuteronomy 1–3’s accounts of Israel’s journey, failures, and God’s faithfulness. Teens engage through moderated Q&A, submitting questions via a live app or notecards for a modern twist.  
**Goals**:  
- Inspire teens to worship fully and connect with Deuteronomy’s narrative.  
- Set a tone of faith and engagement through music and storytelling.  
**Learning Objectives**:  
- Understand worship as a lifestyle of devotion.  
- Experience communal worship as unifying.  
- Grasp the historical context of Deuteronomy 1–3.  
**Activities**:  
- **Visual**: Create a worship collage with images/words reflecting Deut. 6:5.  
- **Auditory**: Participate in live worship music and listen to character narratives.  
- **Kinesthetic**: Engage in a guided prayer walk during worship.  
- **Reading/Writing**: Write a worship commitment and ask panel questions.  
**Facilitator Guidance**:  
- **Edgy Approach**: Kick off with a hype worship setlist (e.g., Lecrae, Hillsong Young & Free) to match teen energy. Use bold visuals (graffiti-style art of Deut. 6:5) projected on screens. For the Q&A, actors should adopt a conversational, almost podcast-like vibe, using modern slang and humor to make Moses and crew relatable (e.g., Moses joking about his staff as his “OG hype stick”).  
- **Relevance**: Connect Israel’s wandering to teens’ struggles with direction (e.g., “Ever feel stuck like the Israelites, scrolling through life but not moving forward?”). Encourage actors to tie character stories to themes like resilience (Joshua), courage (Caleb), or redemption (Aaron). Use a live poll app to let teens vote on which character’s story resonates most, keeping them engaged.  
- **Tips**: Train actors to stay in character but be approachable, answering questions with biblical accuracy and teen-friendly analogies. Keep the worship leader high-energy, encouraging teens to stand, move, or clap to feel the music’s vibe.

**9:30 AM – 10:00 AM: Break & Transition**

**10:00 AM – 11:00 AM: Combined Session – Deuteronomy 4–6:9 – A Quick Overview of God’s Law (60 minutes)**  
**Format**: Scriptural reading with musical accompaniment  
**Description**: The text of Deuteronomy 4–6:9 is read aloud in the style of the Streetlights NLT audio Bible, with volunteer musicians providing live hip-hop or acoustic beats to create an immersive, urban vibe. Visual projections feature graffiti-style key verses (e.g., Shema) synced with the beat. A brief, high-energy reflection by a youth pastor connects God’s law to teen life, emphasizing obedience as “living your best life for God.”  
**Goals**:  
- Introduce the centrality of God’s law.  
- Engage through music and dynamic reading.  
- Emphasize loving and obeying God wholeheartedly.  
**Facilitator Guidance**:  
- **Edgy Approach**: Use a Streetlights-inspired reading with a DJ mixing beats live, dropping bass for key verses like Deut. 6:4–9 to hype the crowd. Project visuals with bold, neon-colored text or animated lyric videos to mimic a concert vibe. The reflection should feel like a TED Talk for teens—short, punchy, with memes or TikTok-style clips illustrating obedience (e.g., a clip of someone choosing honesty over clout).  
- **Relevance**: Frame God’s law as a “life hack” for thriving, not a boring rulebook. Ask provocative questions like, “What if God’s law is the ultimate cheat code for life?” Use examples like peer pressure or social media to show how obedience aligns with God’s plan.  
- **Tips**: Keep the reading dynamic—split verses among multiple readers with distinct voices (e.g., a poet, a rapper). Ensure the reflection is under 5 minutes, using humor and pop culture references to keep teens hooked.
