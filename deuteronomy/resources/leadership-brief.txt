# Brief for Church Leadership Teams: Deuteronomy Conference for Teens

## Overview
We propose a two-day conference for 100 teenage Christians (ages 13–18) to explore the Book of Deuteronomy through engaging, interactive, and culturally relevant sessions. This event will inspire faith, leadership, and worship by connecting ancient Scripture to modern teen life. Volunteers will lead all sessions, and a $55 participant fee will cover food and craft supplies. The conference will be held at [Insert Venue, e.g., Grace Community Church] on [Insert Dates], from 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM daily.

## Purpose and Goals
The conference aims to:
- Deepen teens’ understanding of Deuteronomy’s themes (obedience, leadership, justice, worship).
- Equip teens with practical skills for living out their faith.
- Foster community through dynamic worship, workshops, and fellowship meals.
- Make Scripture relevant using edgy formats like hip-hop, spoken word, and hands-on activities.

## Key Features
- **Combined Sessions**:
  - **Day 1**: Opens with high-energy worship and a Q&A panel with actors portraying Moses, Joshua, Caleb, and Aaron (Deut. 1–3), followed by a Streetlights-style reading of Deut. 4–6:9 with live beats.
  - **Day 2**: Starts with a rhythmic Shema chant (Deut. 6:4–9) and crafts, closing with a dramatized Moses’ farewell (Deut. 31–34) and spoken-word wrap-up.
- **Breakout Sessions** (up to 3 concurrent):
  - Topics include leadership (Deut. 1:9–18), social justice (Deut. 24), tithing (Deut. 15), festivals (Deut. 16), and more, with activities like clay tablet carving, rap writing, and budgeting games.
  - Facilitator guidance ensures sessions are edgy (e.g., podcast-style narratives, TikTok-inspired videos) and relatable (e.g., comparing the Shema to a “daily reset”).
- **Meals**: Buffet-style, blending teen favorites (sliders, pizza, tacos) with themed foods (e.g., “Manna Wraps,” “Milk and Honey Milkshakes”) inspired by Deut. 16’s feasts.
- **Budget**: Total cost of $5,080 (food: $3,600; crafts: $1,250; miscellaneous: $230), covered by $5,500 in participant fees, with a $420 surplus for contingencies or donation.

## Logistics
- **Target Audience**: 100 teens (ages 13–18), plus 20 volunteers.
- **Venue Needs**: Main hall, three breakout rooms, AV equipment (provided by venue), buffet setup.
- **Staffing**: Volunteers (youth pastors, actors, musicians) trained for high-energy, teen-focused delivery.
- **Promotion**: Via youth groups, churches, and social media with vibrant graphics and TikTok-style teasers.

## Expected Outcomes
- Teens will grow in faith, understanding Deuteronomy’s relevance to their lives.
- Participants will develop skills in leadership, stewardship, and justice.
- The event will strengthen youth group community and inspire bold faith.
